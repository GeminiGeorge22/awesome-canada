# Awesome Canada [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of awesome lists about Canada.

Each entry is a list of its own, maintained by its own authors. This list collects them in one place, by topic.

## Contents

- [General](#general)
- [Government and Public Sector](#government-and-public-sector)
- [Money and Travel](#money-and-travel)
- [Cities and Regions](#cities-and-regions)
- [Science and Technology](#science-and-technology)

## General

- [Awesome Canada (wra-sol)](https://github.com/wra-sol/awesome-canada#readme) - Official government digital tools, portals, open data, maps and calculators at every level, from federal to municipal.

## Government and Public Sector

- [Government of Canada Open Source](https://github.com/swill/awesome-gc#readme) - Open source policies, frameworks, libraries and software from the Government of Canada.

## Money and Travel

- [Personal Finance Canada](https://github.com/NickLewanowicz/awesome-personal-finance-canada#readme) - Online resources for managing your money in Canada.
- [Canada Travel](https://github.com/brandonhimpfen/awesome-canada-travel#readme) - Apps, communities, guides and tools for travelling in Canada.

## Cities and Regions

- [Montréal](https://github.com/opensourcecities/montreal#readme) - Open source companies, people and projects from Montréal.
- [Toronto](https://github.com/rodolfobandeira/awesome-toronto#readme) - Tips for getting around and living in Toronto.
- [Vancouver](https://github.com/opensourcecities/vancouver#readme) - Open source companies, people and projects from Vancouver.

## Science and Technology

- [Canadian Robotics](https://github.com/norlab-ulaval/awesome-canadian-robotics#readme) - Canadian robotics open source software, companies and researchers.
- [Wildfire](https://github.com/ubc-lib-geo/awesome-wildfire#readme) - Wildland fire resources from across Canada.
- [Maritimes Tech](https://github.com/adamduval/awesome-maritimes-tech#readme) - The tech scene of New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island.

## Contributing

Know an awesome list about Canada that belongs here? Open a pull request that adds it to the fitting section, with a one-line description ending in a period. Only lists, please: a link to a single tool or site belongs in one of the lists above.
